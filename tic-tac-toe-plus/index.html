Rules (current)
  • Goal: first to reach the match target wins the match. Each round you win = 1 point.
  • Each round: a coin flip decides who goes first.
  • Normal turn: tap an empty square to place your mark.
  • Steal (RPS): tap an opponent's square to challenge them to Rock-Paper-Scissors. Winner takes the square; loser forfeits the turn. Tie → coin flip decides.
  • Round win: three in a row, column, or diagonal. Full board with no winner = no point awarded.
  • More rules will reveal as the match progresses.
